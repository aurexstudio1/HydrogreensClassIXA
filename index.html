About Hydrogreens

Plants grown with water, sold with pride

Hydrogreens is the Class IX-A Kaushal Vikas project. We grow hydroponic plants — Money Plant, Desk Plant, Tulsi, Methi, Sprout, Chia seeds, Lucky Bamboo, Garlic and more — in cans, glass jars and plastic containers, along with the nutrient solution, each with its selling price (SP).

Hydroponics Explained

Farming without soil

Hydroponics grows plants with their roots in water enriched with nutrients instead of soil.

💧

Water

Roots sit in water that carries everything the plant needs.

🧪

Nutrient solution

A few drops of solution (listed in our sheet) feed the plant.

☀️

Light

Sunlight or bright indoor light powers growth.

🌱

Benefits

Uses little space, stays tidy and suits desks and windows.
